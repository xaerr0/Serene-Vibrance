Meet Kristen Irene

Ever since I was young, I've wanted to really know people — to connect with them in a meaningful way. That's what has guided this entire career. For the past 18 years, I've walked alongside women through their inner growth and healing: leading women's circles for friends starting about 13 years ago, and in 2020 growing those circles into coaching groups that became the retreats I lead today. Along the way, I trained at a holistic school in Maui, deepened my Reiki practice (first degree at seven, second-degree at twenty-one), and spent eight years refining my skincare craft at a medical spa. I've had the joy of hearing hundreds of women's deepest thoughts and most beautiful life stories along the way.

Serene Vibrance brings together everything I've learned and loved: expert skincare, energy work, and intuitive counseling. Every facial is personalized, every coaching session meets you exactly where you are, and you'll always feel cared for. Come in carrying it all. Leave replenished and glowing, inside and out.
