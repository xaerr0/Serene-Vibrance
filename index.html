Meet Kristen Irene

With over 20 years in the spa and wellness industry, I've built a career around what I'm most passionate about: helping people feel seen, supported, and radiant. My background includes 18 years as a women's retreat facilitator, training at a holistic school in Maui, 2nd-degree Reiki certification, and extensive medical spa technique training.

Serene Vibrance brings together everything I've learned and loved: expert skincare, energy work, and intuitive guidance. Every facial is personalized, every coaching session is a real conversation, and you'll always feel cared for. Walk in feeling stressed. Walk out feeling like yourself again.
