An Invitation

When it comes to money, so many of us still feel a quiet tightening — that sense that there isn't quite enough, or that you have to work day and night to make it. Whether you have money or you don't, a scarcity mindset can still be running your life.

This circle is a soft place to land and practice something different. For one evening, we'll gather to feel what abundance truly feels like, notice where scarcity has been running the show, and leave with a clear, grounded plan for the six weeks ahead — right up to the end of the year. It's an evening to clear what's in the way — and open to what's on its way.

Your Guides

Kristen Diaz, Intuitive Life Coach, brings 18 years of work with women and 13 years of leading women's circles, along with deep warmth and intuitive guidance.

Assiyah Rouhani, Clinical Hypnotherapist, specializes in subconscious rewiring and nervous system work, helping you release old patterns and step into your authentic power.

What We'll Do Together

  • Live in your abundance.

    Are you feeling your true abundance? Become a wealth magnet when you re-align your thinking to notice all the ways wealth is showing up for you — in opportunities, good fortune, surprises, and acts of generosity.

  • Scarcity vs. abundance.

    You'll learn to recognize where your mindset is serving you or blocking you and make a meaningful shift.

  • Find small ways to be of service.

    Generosity keeps things flowing. We'll gather simple ideas — a glowing Google review for a business you love, a generous testimonial, a love note to a friend, home-baked treats for your coworkers or a friend.

  • Align with the season of light.

    We'll honor this powerful time of year, when Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is a sacred time in the Hindu tradition to welcome in wealth and abundance — and open ourselves to receive it too.

  • Map your next six weeks.

    You'll write out real, specific ideas to increase your income between now and the end of the year — and share them with a circle of women who'll cheer you on.
