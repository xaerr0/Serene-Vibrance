What Is Intuitive Coaching?

Think of it as a conversation with someone who gets it — one centered in the belief that when you slow down enough to feel into your body and get centered, your own inner guidance is already there. Together, we'll follow that guidance beneath the surface to uncover what's really holding you back from living the life you want.

Whether you're stuck, overwhelmed, or just ready for something different, coaching can help you gain clarity, reconnect to your passion, and fall in love with yourself and your life again. Every session — including guided meditation, a healing Reiki treatment, and often home practices to support you in between — is personalized and designed specifically for you.

With 18 years guiding women through inner growth and healing, and over 6 years as a group facilitator, Kristen brings real expertise and authentic care to every session.
