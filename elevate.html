What is Elevate?

Elevate is a 3-month journey designed for heart-centered women who are ready to step into their highest potential. This isn't about information alone. It's about real, embodied transformation that shows up in your life right now.

You'll experience weekly group coaching, two full-day retreats, facials, massage, energy work, and a supportive community of women committed to their own growth. By the end, you'll have clearer boundaries, stronger relationships, deeper self-connection, and a life that actually feels like yours.

Program Dates

September 12 – December 5, 2026

Meeting at Serene Vibrance in Lafayette and Lafayette Reservoir for our full-day retreat experiences.

Limited to 12 women per cohort to ensure everyone receives personalized attention and support.

Program Details

Is Elevate Right for You?

You're a good fit if you're ready to invest in yourself and committed to showing up for your transformation. You don't need to have coaching experience—just a willingness to be honest and grow.

Elevate is designed for women who want more than information. You want embodied change that shows up in your relationships, career, health, and how you feel about yourself.

How to Apply

We take time to make sure Elevate is the right program for you. The application process includes a brief form and a discovery call with Kristen or Assiyah to discuss your goals and answer your questions.

Spots are limited to 12 women per cohort, so we can give everyone the attention they deserve.
