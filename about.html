About Kristen

There are facials, and then there are Facial Experiences. After over 17 years working in the spa industry I have developed relaxing facials that will transform your skin to optimum health that people will notice. Although I started my career working in a medical spa, my facials are a holistic experience. Your facial will be an ultimate experience in de-stressing and pampering with relaxing massage and guided meditation that will nourish you mind, body, and spirit. Warm mitts and booties with a deep moisture treatment will soften and hydrate your hands and feet. Your scalp, face, neck, shoulders, arms, and feet will be rubbed and relaxed extensively. You will positively float out of the treatment room. Each facial includes the most cutting edge technology and skin care ingredients on the market. Not only do I target the skin surface and collagen level, but there is also a toning and sculpting facial lift included in most facial treatments to firm your skin at the deepest level possible. This is not just important for the aging client, but also healing and detoxifying for the congested skin clients as well.
You will feel safe and nurtured in my expertise and care, and you will see your skin restored, balanced, and radiant.
